YUKI COMPANION — START HERE

Please do these steps in order. You only need to do them once.

1. Open the folder you downloaded and double-click YukiCompanion.exe.
2. Follow the setup window. When it asks what Yuki can see:
   - Open Minecraft first.
   - Press “Refresh open windows”.
   - Choose the row that says Minecraft and the game window title.
   - Do not choose Minecraft Launcher.
3. Open Microsoft Edge and type edge://extensions in the address bar.
   Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and choose the EdgeExtension
   folder that came with this download.
4. Open ChatGPT in Edge. Open the chat called “Yuki — App Companion”.
   Click the Yuki extension button and choose “Bind this tab to Yuki”.
5. Go back to Yuki. Type a message. Press the eye button when you want her to
   look at Minecraft.

If Minecraft is not in the list:
   Keep Minecraft open, open Yuki’s Settings, press Refresh, and choose the
   Minecraft game window. If you are unsure, choose the row with Minecraft in
   its name but not the words Launcher or Yuki.

If something goes wrong, do not reinstall everything. First close and reopen
Yuki, refresh the Edge extension, refresh the ChatGPT page, and bind the tab
again. More help is in ChatGPT-Setup-Prompt.txt and README-Windows.md.
